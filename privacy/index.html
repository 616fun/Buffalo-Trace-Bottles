Privacy Policy

Effective September 22, 2026  ·  buffalotracebottledrops.com

The short version. The bottle tracker itself collects nothing personal about you. There are no accounts, no cookies set by us, no advertising trackers, and no analytics that follow you anywhere else. You can read the whole site without telling us who you are.

The one place we do collect personal information is the optional SMS alert waitlist, where you choose to give us your name, mobile number and date of birth. That is covered in detail below.

1. Who operates this site

buffalotracebottledrops.com is an independently operated hobby project that tracks publicly posted bottle availability at the Buffalo Trace Distillery gift shop in Frankfort, Kentucky. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Buffalo Trace Distillery or Sazerac Company, Inc.

Questions about this policy, or requests about your data, go to alerts@buffalotracebottledrops.com.

2. Visiting the tracker

If you only browse the site, we do not ask for or store any information that identifies you. Specifically:

  • No accounts or logins. There is nothing to sign up for to use the tracker.
  • No cookies set by us, and nothing stored in your browser's local storage.
  • No advertising or cross-site tracking. We run no ad network, no retargeting pixels, and no social media trackers. We do not sell data, because we do not have any to sell.

Aggregate traffic measurement

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views and see which pages get read. It is cookieless and does not fingerprint or follow visitors between sites. We see totals — how many views a page got, roughly where visitors came from, desktop versus mobile — not individuals.

Security and delivery logs

The site is served through Cloudflare, which processes your IP address in order to deliver pages and to protect the site from abuse and denial-of-service attacks. This is ordinary infrastructure logging that happens for any website behind a CDN, and is handled under Cloudflare's own privacy terms. We do not use these logs to build profiles of visitors.

Third parties your browser contacts

Some page assets load from other companies' servers. When that happens, your browser reveals your IP address to them, as it does for any site using the same services:

ServiceWhat it is
CloudflareHosting, CDN, bot protection, analytics

We receive nothing back from these services about you individually.

3. The SMS alert waitlist

This is optional and separate from using the tracker. If you choose to join it, we collect:

WhatWhy
First name (and last name, if you give it)So messages and support replies aren't anonymous
Mobile phone numberTo send the alerts you asked for. This is the whole point.
Date of birthTo verify you are 21 or older. Text message programs about alcohol are required to verify age, and a checkbox is not sufficient. It is kept as the record of that check and is never used for marketing.
Sign-up date and time, IP address, browser user agent, time zone To prove when and from where you consented, if that is ever questioned
The exact consent wording shown to you So there is a permanent record of precisely what you agreed to, even if the wording later changes

How we use it

  • To send you the daily availability alert you signed up for, and nothing else.
  • To answer you if you reply HELP or email us.
  • To demonstrate, if required, that you gave consent before we messaged you.

What we never do

  • We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your phone number with anyone for marketing.
  • We do not use your number for any program other than this one.
  • We do not text you about anything except bottle availability.
  • We do not share your date of birth with anyone, for any reason.

Who else touches it

Messages are delivered by Twilio, which necessarily processes your phone number to deliver them, under its own privacy terms. Sign-up records are stored on Cloudflare infrastructure. No one else receives your information.

How long we keep it

We keep your record while you are subscribed. When you reply STOP or ask to be removed, we stop messaging you immediately and mark your record inactive. We retain a suppression entry — your number and the fact that you opted out — so that you are never accidentally re-added without asking us to be. If you want your record deleted entirely rather than marked inactive, email us and we will do it.

Nobody has been texted yet. The alert program is currently a waitlist. If it launches, everyone on the list is contacted first, and you can opt out at that point without ever receiving a second message.

4. If paid subscriptions are introduced

The tracker is free and is intended to stay free. If a paid version of the SMS alerts is ever offered, payments would be handled entirely by an established payment processor. Card numbers would go directly to that processor and never touch this site — we would see only whether a subscription is active. This policy would be updated before any such change takes effect.

5. Your choices

  • Stop texts: reply STOP to any message. This works immediately and needs no explanation.
  • Get help: reply HELP, or email alerts@buffalotracebottledrops.com.
  • See, correct, or delete what we hold: email us and ask. We will respond.
  • Use the tracker without any of this: always available, no sign-up, no conditions.

6. Age

This site concerns alcohol product availability. It is intended for adults of legal drinking age. The SMS alert program is restricted to people 21 years of age or older and to United States mobile numbers. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 21. If you believe someone underage has signed up, email us and we will remove the record.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top changes with it. If the change materially affects people already on the SMS list — for example, a new use of their information — they will be notified before it takes effect.
