1. What this service is

buffalotracebottledrops.com records and analyses publicly posted bottle availability at the Buffalo Trace Distillery gift shop in Frankfort, Kentucky. Availability data is read once daily from the distillery's own public product availability page. Predictions are generated by a statistical model from that history.

2. No affiliation

This site is operated independently. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or connected to Buffalo Trace Distillery or Sazerac Company, Inc. All product names and trademarks belong to their respective owners and are used only to describe what is being tracked.

3. Accuracy, and what it cannot promise

The information here is provided for general interest, as-is, with no warranty of any kind. In particular:

  • Data reflects what the distillery posted at the time of the daily check. Shelves change throughout the day, and bottles listed as available frequently sell out within hours.
  • Predictions are statistical estimates, not promises. A high predicted probability is not a guarantee that anything will be on the shelf.
  • The distillery's own page is occasionally stale or incomplete. Where we detect that, the day is flagged, but we cannot correct data we do not have.
  • Special releases can appear and sell out entirely between daily checks and never show up here.
Always confirm with the gift shop before making a trip. Do not rely on this site as the sole basis for travel, purchase, or any other decision. We are not responsible for a wasted drive.
